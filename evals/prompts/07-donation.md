# Coastal Reading Foundation — Transparency Ledger & Island Book Drive (EN)

Build a production-ready single-page website, as well as you can, for the transparency report and book-drive campaign of "Coastal Reading Foundation", a nonprofit establishing village libraries across Eastern Indonesia's outer islands. Prospective donors need to review the annual fund allocation report (percentage spent on children's books, cargo freight, local librarian training, and administration), an audited delivery table covering 6 target villages with book counts and arrival status, and a one-time or monthly pledge simulator calculator and form clearly labeled as a simulation with no live payment charge.
