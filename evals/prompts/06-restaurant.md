# Kedai & Roastery Jati — Coffee Brew Bar & Sourdough Bakery Bandung (EN)

Build a production-ready single-page website, as well as you can, for "Kedai & Roastery Jati", a single-origin Nusantara coffee brew bar and wood-fired sourdough bakery at Jl. Taman Cibeunying Selatan No. 14, Bandung. Prospective guests need to inspect the complete manual-brew, espresso, loose-leaf tea, and morning sourdough menu with bean origins, tasting notes, and real prices (Rp24,000 to Rp46,000), the weekly roasting schedule, daily opening hours (07:00–21:00 WIB), desk and power-outlet availability, walk-in seating rules for 1–3 guests without reservation, and a group-table booking form for parties of 4–8 guests.
