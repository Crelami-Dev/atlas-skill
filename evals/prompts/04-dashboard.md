# NusaKirim — Daily Dispatch & COD Cash Operations Console (EN)

Build a production-ready single-page website, as well as you can, for the daily operational console "NusaKirim" used by regional logistics coordinators to monitor parcel dispatches and daily COD cash collections. Operators need summary telemetry of collected COD cash, total active parcels, and delayed shipments; a searchable and filterable daily dispatch ledger showing tracking number, destination city, courier, COD amount, and delivery status (Delivered, In Transit, Delayed, Failed); the active courier shift roster; and a detail slide-over log panel whenever a tracking row is selected.
