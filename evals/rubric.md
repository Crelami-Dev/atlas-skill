# Blind scoring rubric

Score each criterion 1–5: **1** unusable/generic or unsupported, **3** competent with clear gaps, **5** deliberate, evidence-backed, and task-appropriate. Score hierarchy, typography, distinctiveness, system consistency, responsiveness, basic accessibility, and content honesty.
